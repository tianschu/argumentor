论证工坊 · ArguMentor
Version 4.0.0  (2026-10-02)
A multi-agent Socratic coach for English argumentative writing (EFL),
with a software integrity guard, Socratic dialogue, a closing feedback
loop, teacher tools and a built-in evaluation harness.

Requires Node.js 20.3 or newer. No third-party dependencies.
