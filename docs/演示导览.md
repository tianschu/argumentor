**哪些是真实的，哪些是模拟的。** 学生是模拟的——由 AI 助手扮演的一名 B2 水平英语专业二年级学生。但录制中的**每一条智能体输出、每一次苏格拉底追问、每一次修订检查，都是真实的 DeepSeek 响应**，于 2026 年 10 月 2 日实时捕获（`deepseek-flash`，提示词版本 `38bd27ec863f`；两轮共 19 次模型调用）。事后没有做任何修改。回放为只读模式，界面横幅会明确说明。

## 第 1 步 · 明确任务

教师任务《Unit 5 · AI monitoring in the workplace》已载入，并**锁定**了议题与读者（“一家中型物流公司的管理层与员工代表”）。任务附带三份材料：两份教师编写的虚构案例，一份教师整理的数据保护原则说明；另有十条议题词汇，正反双方均可使用。

学生最初的主张：

> “Employers should not use AI systems to monitor their employees, because it is an invasion of privacy and it destroys the trust between workers and managers.”

没有填写限定。（这一点后面会回来。）

---

## 第 2 步 · 构建论证：第一件发生的事与 AI 无关

两条理由，各引用一份材料。学生把理由 2 的证据写成：

> In Case A the system *“flagged workers who helped their colleagues as lazy”*.

而材料原文是 *“flagged workers who stopped to help a colleague as idle”*。学生凭记忆引用，记错了——这再正常不过。

工具立即标出：

> ✗ 引文未在材料中找到 S1 —— *“flagged workers who helped their colleagues as lazy”*

**没有调用任何模型。** 这是 `checkSourceQuotes()` 在把引号内的词与所注明的材料逐词比对。学生在请求反馈之前就改正了，标记随即变绿。“核查来源”从一句建议变成了一项确定性检查。

## 第 3 步 · 追问对话

### 先自评

运行之前，学生先自己指出最薄弱之处：

> **理由 2 · 推理联系** —— “I did not explain why one mistake in Case A means that AI monitoring is unfair in general.”
> 想问教练：“Is my response to the counterargument strong enough?”

### 四个智能体，4 次调用，8.7 秒

**论证分析** —— 先给结构化检查，再给带锚定的观察：

| | |
|---|---|
| 主张 | 回应了议题 · **范围过大** |
| 理由 1 | 教师材料 · 证据链接*部分说明* · 主张链接**未说明** |
| 理由 2 | 教师材料 · 证据链接**未说明** · 主张链接**未说明** |
| 异议 1 | 公正呈现 · 回应类型*反驳* |

它首先回应学生的自评：

> *“You correctly identified the missing warrant for Reason 2 as the most important issue. Your question about the counterargument is also relevant, but the warrant comes first.”*

并引用学生原话，指出一处**值得保留**：

> *“If good employees leave, company will lose talent and money”* —— “You connect the evidence to a concrete consequence for the company. This shows you are thinking about why the audience should care.”

（学生的语法疏漏 “company will lose” 被原样保留——分析角色不管语言。）

**苏格拉底式追问** —— 三个带类型、带锚定的问题：

- **S1**（追问证据，理由 2）：“What would a manager need to know about how common this kind of AI mistake is before accepting that it makes monitoring unfair?”
- **S2**（其他解释，理由 2）：“If a company fixed the AI so it no longer flagged helpful workers, would your reason against monitoring still stand?”
- **S3**（前后对照，主张）：“Your claim rejects all AI monitoring, yet your counterargument admits monitoring can warn drivers who drive too fast; how do these two positions fit together?”

S3 是那种不需要任何外部事实的招法：它把学生**自己的**两句话并排放在一起。

**语言教练** —— 指向学生自己的句子，绝不改写：

- **L1**（立场强度，初稿）：针对 *“As we all know, privacy is a basic right”* —— “This appeal to shared knowledge asks the board to accept a contested value as obvious.”
- **L2**（立场强度，理由 2）：针对 *“so it is totally unfair to use it to judge people”* —— “The booster ‘totally’ is much stronger than one example from Case A can support.”

**修订协调** —— 两条重点，各自标明来源条目与自查问题：

- **R1**（来自 A1、S1、L2）：处理理由 2 的推理联系。*自查：“Does my warrant explain why this example justifies rejecting AI monitoring, not just fixing it?”*
- **R2**（来自 A2、S3）：把主张与异议对照。*自查：“Does my claim's scope match the cases my evidence covers?”*

它还如实指出了张力：

> “The analyst wants a narrower claim, while your counterargument already concedes a use of monitoring; narrowing the claim would resolve both, but it changes the position you set out to defend.”

### 对话 —— 学习真正发生的地方

**S1 第 1 轮。** 学生：*“I think the manager would need to know how often the system makes this mistake. In Case A it happened in the first month, but I don't know if it continued after the company changed the rule.”*

教练（**深入追问**）：“If the company fixed the rule after the first month, would that change whether monitoring is unfair? Explain how the fix affects your reason.”

**第 2 轮。** 学生：*“Hmm, after the fix a supervisor checks the score before any action, so one wrong flag would not punish a worker directly. **Maybe the real problem is using AI scores without a human check, not monitoring itself.** But workers could still feel watched all the time.”*

教练（**确认发现**，引用经核实的学生原话）：“That is a sharper claim than your draft's. Your draft says employers should not monitor at all. Would you narrow your claim to unchecked AI scoring, or keep the wider one?”

**第 3 轮。** 学生：*“I think I will narrow it. I still believe that constant AI tracking of every worker is wrong, but I can accept monitoring for safety if a person reviews the results and workers can see their own data.”*

教练（**小结**）：用学生自己的话概括其立场，并建议把它带入主张与对安全异议的回应。

**改变了这篇文章的那句话，是学生写的，不是 AI 写的。** 教练只问了三个问题，并把学生的话引回给他自己。

![追问对话](../screenshots/v4/03-socratic-dialogue.jpg)

学生还对 L2 开了一条**澄清线程**：*“I am not sure what a hedge is here. Do you mean that I should not give my opinion so strongly?”* 教练（**解释反馈**）回答：

> “A hedge means softening a claim to fit your evidence. Your word ‘totally’ says AI is always unfair, but Case A is only one example. Hedging is not hiding your opinion; it keeps it believable. Which part of Reason 2 could you soften to match one example?”

先解释，再提问。依然不代写。

## 第 4 步 · 自主修订

修订重点、学生在对话中的发现、以及编辑器同屏呈现。每条重点都必须作出决定：

| | 决定 | 学生写的理由 |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | 采纳 | “I will explain that unchecked scores can punish helpful workers, so the real problem is scoring without a human check.” |
| R2 | 调整后采纳 | “I will narrow my claim to productivity scoring, but I will keep my position against constant tracking of every worker.” |
| L1 | 调整后采纳 | “I will keep privacy as an important value, but I will present it as my own view, not as something everyone knows.” |
| L2 | 采纳 | “I will remove ‘totally’ because one example cannot prove that AI is always unfair.” |

学生改写后为 329 词，相对初稿**新增 271 词、删除 114 词**（以词级差异呈现）。新的主张：

> “Employers should not use AI to score the productivity of individual workers and judge them by these scores, unless a person reviews every score and workers can see their own data.”

## 第 5 步 · 检查反思

修订检查（1 次调用）给出结论，不打分：

- **R1 · 已体现** —— 引用修订稿：*“This example does not prove that AI is always wrong. However, it shows that a score can punish exactly the teamwork that a warehouse needs”* —— “推理联系现在把这个例子连向了‘拒绝无人复核的评分’，而不是‘修好这个工具’。”
- **R2 · 已体现** —— “主张现在点名了生产效率评分，并加入了人工复核条件。安全提醒这一异议被让步，并按用途作了区分。”

向前看的问题：*“In your next draft, how will you show that the human-review condition is realistic for a mid-sized logistics company?”*

## 第 2 轮 —— 闭环，也包括学生说“不”的权利

学生点击“用修订稿开始第 2 轮”。初稿被替换，论证地图同步更新，并写下新的自评：

> **理由 1 · 推理联系** —— “I am not sure the board will accept that two designers leaving a studio is a real cost for a logistics company.”

变更面板列出自第 1 轮以来改动的全部字段：主张、限定、初稿、两条理由、两条推理联系、回应策略。

**分析角色再次同意学生的判断**——而这一次，问题是学生先发现的：Case B 是设计工作室，而读者经营的是仓库。追问角色跟进后，学生自己得出：*“Counting cannot show helping a colleague or training a new worker, and Case A shows exactly that”*——因此 Case A 应当承担物流公司这条论证，Case B 只用来说明“被监视的感受”。

接下来是有意思的部分。对 **R2**（让主张的措辞显示安全提醒不在其范围内），学生点了**不采纳**：

> “My qualifier already says that safety alerts are outside my claim, so I will keep the claim wording as it is.”

第 2 轮的修订检查记录为：

- **R1 · 部分体现** —— “设计工作室证据与物流公司之间的差距现在被点明并部分弥合。推理联系仍未说明：对仓库员工而言，什么条件成立才会让‘失去有经验的员工’构成这家董事会应当在意的真实成本。”
- **R2 · 学生决定不采纳** —— “学生拒绝了这条重点，理由是限定句已经把安全提醒排除在主张之外。主张措辞与让步均未改动，因此范围完全由限定句承担。”

它描述了学生这一选择带来的后果，**而不与之争辩**。若有第 3 轮，系统会收到这个决定，不会再次提出 R2。

## 教师拿到什么

导出的记录（HTML 或 JSON，带校验码）包含两轮全过程：**当时被分析的那一版论证**、每条带核实引文的智能体条目、完整对话、每条带理由的决定、两份修订稿及差异、两次修订检查、可迁移策略，以及由过程记录草拟、经学生确认的 AI 使用说明：

> “I used ArguMentor, an AI writing coach (model: deepseek-flash), for 2 round(s) of feedback… It asked me questions and pointed out issues; it did not write text for my essay. I wrote 9 replies in dialogue with the coach and made 8 decisions about its feedback (accepted 4, adapted 3, rejected 1). All sentences in my final text are my own, and I can explain every change I made.”

在「教师」页面导入一批这样的记录，即可得到全班视图：追问回答数、决定分布、修订检查结果，以及可用于课堂讨论的全班追问与学生发现，并可导出 CSV。
